Required Permissions

1. Assign Role on Azure AI Search
Grant your VM's Managed Identity permission to read, write, and create index objects in AI Search:
1. Go to your **Azure AI Search** resource -> **Access Control (IAM)** -> **+ Add role assignment**.
2. Search for the role **`Search Index Data Contributor`** and select it.
3. Under the **Members** tab, set **Assign access to** to **Managed identity**.
4. Click **+ Select members**, select your VM (or User-Assigned Identity), and click **Select**.
5. Click **Review + assign**.

#### 2. Assign Role on Azure AI Foundry / OpenAI

Grant your VM's Managed Identity permission to call model endpoints and generate vector embeddings:

1. Go to your **Azure AI Foundry / Azure OpenAI** resource -> **Access Control (IAM)** -> **+ Add role assignment**.
2. Search for the role **`Cognitive Services OpenAI User`** and select it.
3. Under the **Members** tab, set **Assign access to** to **Managed identity**.
4. Click **+ Select members**, select your VM (or User-Assigned Identity), and click **Select**.
5. Click **Review + assign**.

#### 3. Assign Role on Azure Blob Storage

Grant your VM's Managed Identity permission to read the JSON files from Blob Storage:

1. Go to your Storage Account **`txrhragsnowkb`** -> **Access Control (IAM)** -> **+ Add role assignment**.
2. Search for the role **`Storage Blob Data Contributor`** and select it.
3. Under **Members**, select your VM's Managed Identity.
4. Click **Review + assign**.
